# Approved sound sources

Approved by the project owner: audition 01, 03B gunshot, 08, 09.

ServiceRifle.ogg and HuntingRifleMosin.ogg: Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, Matthew Nanney, The Free Firearm Sound Library (CC0). Source AR-15/D_32P.wav and Mosin Nagant/M_26P.wav. Trimmed, high-pass filtered, gain adjusted and encoded to OGG.
https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library

RifleReload.ogg: SpringySpringo, Gun reload sounds (CC0), assaultriflereload1_0.wav. Trimmed/gain adjusted/encoded.
https://opengameart.org/content/gun-reload-sounds

FieldWind.ogg: Luke.RUSTLTD, wind1 (CC0), synthesized wind. Ten-second audition excerpt, gain/fades, encoded to OGG.
https://opengameart.org/content/wind1

CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Rejected audition sounds are not included. The pump-action handling placeholder in 03B is not included; the approved rifle shot is used alone.
